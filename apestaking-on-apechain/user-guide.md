#### 🦍 NFT Stakers (including BAYC, MAYC, and BAKC)

NFT stakers are users who stake their NFTs through BendDAO, pairing them with ApeCoin in the BendDAO ApeCoin pool to earn ApeCoin yields.

**Step 1:** Unstake from the old NFT pools ([https://v1.benddao.xyz/en/ape-staking/pool-staking/my-stakings/nft-pool/withdraw/](https://v1.benddao.xyz/en/ape-staking/pool-staking/my-stakings/nft-pool/withdraw/))

**Step 2:** Deposit the NFT in the new NFT pools ([https://apechain.benddao.xyz/en/](https://apechain.benddao.xyz/en/))

#### 🪙 ApeCoin Stakers

**Step 1:** Unstake from the ApeCoin pool on Ethereum ([https://v1.benddao.xyz/en/ape-staking/pool-staking/my-stakings/apecoin-pool/withdraw/](https://v1.benddao.xyz/en/ape-staking/pool-staking/my-stakings/apecoin-pool/withdraw/))

**Step 2:** Bridge ApeCoin to ApeChain ([https://apechain.com/portal](https://apechain.com/portal))

**Step 3:** Stake the ApeCoin in the new ApeCoin pool on ApeChain ([https://apechain.benddao.xyz/](https://apechain.benddao.xyz/))

#### Borrowers (including BAYC, and MAYC)

Users who take loans against their BAYC/MAYC can still delegate via BendDAO to enable the Shadow NFT on ApeChain for staking participation. However, the new BendDAO ApeStaking on ApeChain does not support pairing services, which means users need to provide the ApeCoin themselves.
